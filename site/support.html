Support

Something not working, or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Pair the iPhone app

  • On your Mac, open Odin, then Settings, Connections, Phone, and turn on Phone access. A QR code appears.
  • Open the Camera on your iPhone and point it at the code. Tap Open in Odin.
  • The code works once, for 10 minutes. If it has expired, close Settings and open it again for a new one.

The app says "This phone is no longer paired"

The phone was removed in Settings, or Odin was unpaired from all phones. Scan a new QR code to pair again.

The board doesn't load away from home

  • Odin must be running on your Mac, and the Mac must be awake and online.
  • Close the app fully and open it again. It tries your home network first, then the relay, which can take a few seconds.

Remove a phone

In Odin, open Settings, Connections, Phone, and remove the phone from Paired phones. It is cut off at once. Use this if a phone is lost.

Install or update Odin

See Install in the README. Odin updates itself and shows a banner when a new release is out.
